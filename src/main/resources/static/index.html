Events at SFU

SFU features a vibrant campus life with diverse year-round events including career fairs, cultural festivals, academic seminars, and major campus traditions. Students can discover upcoming activities, register for workshops, and track involvement through the central SFU Events Calendar.

Clubs at SFU

SFU offers over 100 student-run clubs covering academics, tech teams, culture, and hobbies managed by the Simon Fraser Student Society. You can explore and join them online via the SFSS Portal or meet them in person during Clubs Days at the start of each semester.
